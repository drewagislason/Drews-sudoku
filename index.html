ᛋᚢᛞᛟᚲᚢViking Sudoku
Pick a rune, then click a square.

Rules

  • Each row, column, and 3×3 box must hold every rune exactly once: ᚠ ᚢ ᚦ ᚨ ᚱ ᚲ ᛝ ᛞ ᛟ
  • Pick a rune in the rune bar, then click a square to place it.
  • The blank tile erases a square.
  • Given (starting) runes are shaded and can't be changed.
  • Conflicting runes turn red. Fill the grid correctly to win.
